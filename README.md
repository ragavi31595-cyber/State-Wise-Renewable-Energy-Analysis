State-Wise Renewable Energy Analysis (2019–2025)

Project Overview

This project analyses renewable energy generation across Indian states and regions from 2019 to 2025. It focuses on wind energy, solar energy, and other renewable energy sources to identify generation trends, regional differences, and energy source contributions.

Problem Statement

Renewable energy generation varies across states and energy sources. Analysing this data helps identify high-performing states, understand generation patterns, and support better renewable energy planning.

Objectives

Analyze state-wise renewable energy generation.

Compare wind, solar, and other renewable energy sources.

Identify yearly and monthly generation trends.

Measure the contribution of each energy source.

Identify high-performing states and regional variations.

Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning and transformation.

Python: Data analysis, statistical analysis, correlation, skewness, and outlier detection.

Power BI: Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

Dataset Description

The dataset includes the following columns:

Date

State Name

State Code

Region

Wind Energy

Solar Energy

Other Renewable Energy

Total Renewable Energy

Project Workflow

Collected and explored renewable energy data.

Cleaned and transformed the dataset using Excel.

Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) using Python.

Analysed energy source contributions, trends, correlations, and outliers.

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Key Analysis

State-wise and region-wise renewable energy generation.

Wind, solar, and other renewable energy contributions.

Monthly and yearly generation trends.

Statistical distribution and outlier analysis.

Comparison of renewable energy performance across states.

Key Insights

The analysis helps identify differences in renewable energy generation across states and energy sources. It supports understanding of generation trends, source-wise contributions, and opportunities to improve renewable energy utilization.

Recommendations

Encourage wind energy development in locations with suitable wind conditions.

Promote rooftop solar installations and public awareness of solar energy.

Explore biogas and other renewable energy opportunities in agricultural regions.

Use generation trends to support regional energy planning.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel, Python, and Power BI can be used together to clean, analyse, and visualize renewable energy data. The findings can support data-driven decisions for renewable energy development and planning.
